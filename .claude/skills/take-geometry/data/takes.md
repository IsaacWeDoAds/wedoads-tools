## What the spread proves

- hair top ranges **360 → 760** across takes — a 400px spread.
- head cx ranges **310 → 596** against a 540 frame centre — he is almost never centred.
- solved scale ranges **1.0 → 1.18**, and is at the `dx ≤ 0` floor in most rows.
- on **7 of 26** takes there is no usable band below the chin at all.

This is why nothing transfers, and why a 3-frame fit fails.
